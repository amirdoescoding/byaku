Face scan: mesh mask prototype

The mesh is MediaPipe's canonical face, decimated to 160 vertices (ml/scan_mesh.py). Live mode keeps only the 76 points Vision gives the app, adds Vision-sized noise and runs at the app's 15 fps, so what feels right here carries over. No camera pixels are drawn. Mirrored: turn to your left and the mask turns to the screen's left.
