Two modes

At your desk, or away from it.

Guard, while you work. Someone looks, and byaku shows a small banner, tints the screen edges, or dims the screen.

Away, when you step out. Anyone looks, and byaku sounds an alarm and locks your Mac.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your Mac.

Each frame is checked once, then dropped. The app never goes online. This site counts visits, with no cookies.

How it works

One frame a second.

Apple's Vision finds faces. A small model on the Neural Engine tells yours from everyone else's. The target is under 1.5 W extra.

Install

Three steps.

1. Download and drag byaku to Applications.

2. Open it. macOS says it can't verify the app.

3. System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway.

FAQ

Questions.

Is the camera always recording?

No. Each frame is checked and dropped.

Does it work in the dark?

Less well.

Why does macOS say it can't verify byaku?

The app isn't signed with a paid Developer ID yet.
